# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Ground a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing the actual impact of behavior.
- Give and accept technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy, consent, boundaries, and requests to stop.
- Acknowledge mistakes, repair harm where possible, and learn from the experience.
- Put the project and community’s long-term interests ahead of individual status.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, stalking, or sustained
  disruption
- Sexualized language, imagery, or attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal/political attacks
- Publishing or threatening to publish another person’s private information
- Deliberately exposing credentials, private source, transcripts, or security report
  contents
- Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents Ground
in public spaces, including repository activity, discussions, events, and project
communications.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not report conduct incidents in a public issue. During the project’s
single-maintainer bootstrap, use Ground’s
[private reporting form](https://github.com/AlphaBetSoup789/ground/security/advisories/new)
and begin the summary with `Conduct report:`. Although GitHub presents this as a
vulnerability form, Ground accepts conduct reports there so the reporter and
repository owner can communicate privately. Conduct reports are not published as
security advisories.

Reports should include the minimum necessary context. Maintainers will protect the
reporter’s privacy as far as practical, avoid conflicts of interest, and respond
proportionately. Depending on severity and history, consequences may include a
private correction, warning, temporary restriction, permanent ban, or referral to
the relevant platform or authorities.

The bootstrap recipient is
[@AlphaBetSoup789](https://github.com/AlphaBetSoup789), Ground’s repository owner
and initial maintainer. Ground cannot offer independent review while it has one
maintainer. If a report concerns that maintainer or conduct on GitHub, reporters
may also use GitHub’s platform reporting and support channels. The project will
add an independent recipient before declaring a stable release.

Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the conduct
process.
